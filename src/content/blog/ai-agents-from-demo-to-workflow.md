AI agent is one of the most common product terms in 2025. In practice, it usually means a model that can do more than answer a question: it can break down a goal, call tools, read files, search information, execute steps, and report results.

## Why it matters

Chatbots are mainly about information exchange. Agents are about task execution. Once AI can reliably call tools, it can enter real business workflows such as research, data cleanup, code changes, customer support, and operations automation.

## Potential impact

- AI product design will need stronger permission controls, confirmation steps, rollback paths, and execution logs.
- Workflow automation platforms will become more deeply connected with large language models.
- Users will move from writing prompts to defining goals, constraints, and acceptance criteria.

## Takeaway

The hard part is not creating an impressive demo; it is reliably completing work in messy real environments. The best near-term agent use cases are bounded, verifiable, and reversible.
