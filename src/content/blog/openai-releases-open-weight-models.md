OpenAI released two open-weight language models in 2025: `gpt-oss-120b` and `gpt-oss-20b`. Unlike fully hosted APIs, open-weight models can be downloaded, deployed, evaluated, and customized inside a developer or enterprise environment.

## Why it matters

Open-weight models have become an important part of the AI ecosystem. Teams care about data control, deployment cost, latency, offline availability, and the ability to fine-tune systems for specific workflows. OpenAI joining this area again makes the open model market more competitive.

## Potential impact

- Enterprises can test AI capabilities in more controlled environments.
- Developers can build vertical applications around customer support, knowledge bases, coding assistants, and internal search.
- Competition may push inference cost, deployment tooling, and evaluation practices forward.

## Takeaway

Open weights do not remove engineering complexity. Teams still need compute, monitoring, security review, and model evaluation. Smaller models are easier for individual developers to experiment with, while larger models are better suited to controlled enterprise infrastructure.
